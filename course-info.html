CourseInfo

Discover one of South Florida's most enjoyable municipal golf courses

There are five sets of tees for all 18 holes as the course stretches from 3,783 yards (Forward Green Tees) to 6,347 yards (Back Black Tees), with lakes and contoured greens offering a challenge for golfers of most skill levels. And, the greens are very interesting, we call them wicked fun due to their movement and unique design.

Located in a convenient location in Davie, on Nova Drive between Pine Island Road and University Drive. About 15 minutes from the airport, and 25 minutes from the beach. A very fun activity for both locals and travelers.

Pro Shop Info

  • Pro Shop is open daily from 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM
  • Course closes 15 minutes before sunset
  • Tee times available up to 7 days in advance
  • Snack bar is located in the pro shop
  • Personal alcohol beverages brought from home not allowed
