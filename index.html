Welcome to Davie Golf Club

The course was redesigned in 2011 and new renovated greens in 2024 to enhance its enjoyment and to provide an exciting challenge for all levels of ability. Several hundred native trees have been planted to provide a definition of each hole's landing area and green site. Harrison Minchew is the primary architect. The course was designed to the same standard of design that Mr. Minchew used while working as Arnold Palmer's Senior Golf Course Architect. The course consists of rolling fairways and multi-tiered greens.
